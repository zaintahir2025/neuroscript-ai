# NeuroScript AI Student User Guide

Welcome to NeuroScript AI! This plain-language walkthrough is designed for university students. It explains how to turn your handwritten or printed lecture notes into clear, grounded flashcards and interactive concept maps tailored to your learning style.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Onboarding

When you first launch NeuroScript AI, you will see the welcome screen outlining how the app protects your privacy and supports your study sessions.

![NeuroScript AI Onboarding Screen](images/onboarding.png)

* Tap **Get Started** at the bottom of the screen to proceed to profile setup.
* All your notes stay encrypted on your phone by default.

---

## 2. Choosing Your Learning Profile

NeuroScript AI provides three tailored learning profiles. You can change your choice anytime in Settings; this selection is not a medical diagnosis.

![Profile Selection Screen](images/profile_selection.png)

* **ADHD:** Designed for rapid dopamine feedback and executive function support. Limits study sessions to **5 cards at a time** so you never feel overwhelmed.
* **Dyslexia:** Uses a soft, warm cream background to reduce eye strain, paired with relaxed letter spacing and line heights for comfortable reading.
* **Autism:** Uses calm, soothing pastel colors, predictable layouts, and gentle transitions to minimize sensory overload.
* Select your preferred profile and tap **Save & Continue**.

---

## 3. The Study Library

The Library is your home dashboard where all your study units and courses live.

![Study Library Screen](images/library_dyslexia.png)

* **Seed Sets:** If you are opening the app for the first time, you will find three pre-loaded study sets (*Photosynthesis Fundamentals*, *CPU Pipeline & Memory Hierarchy*, *Binary Search & Divide-and-Conquer*) so you can start studying right away.
* **Due Today:** Each card indicates how many flashcards are due for review according to your spaced repetition schedule.
* **Quick Actions:** Tap **Concept Map** on any card to view its visual graph, or tap the three dots for options (e.g. deleting or renaming).

---

## 4. Capturing or Importing Lecture Notes

Ready to add your own lecture notes? You can photograph handwritten pages directly with your camera or import existing photos from your gallery.

![Capture Viewfinder Screen](images/capture_viewfinder.png)

* Tap the **Scan Note** button (or the camera icon in the top bar) from the Library.
* **Framing Guide:** Align your paper notes within the rectangular viewfinder.
* **Multi-Page Support:** Take up to 5 consecutive photos for a multi-page lecture topic.
* **Gallery Import:** Tap **Gallery** to select clear photos from your photo library.
* When finished, tap **Review OCR** to transcribe your notes into text.

---

## 5. Reviewing & Editing Transcribed Text

Before creating flashcards, NeuroScript AI lets you inspect and edit the text extracted from your notes.

![OCR Line Review Screen](images/ocr_review.png)

* **Line Numbers (`L1`, `L2`, ...):** Every line of text is assigned a permanent number so future flashcards can cite the exact line back to your notes.
* **Inline Editing:** Tap any line to correct a typo or misspelling.
* **Merge & Reorder:** Merge lines that were split across pages or delete stray handwriting marks.
* **Generate Lesson:** When you are satisfied with the text, tap **Generate Lesson**. Your phone securely compiles the notes into a structured study set.

---

## 6. Lesson Overview & Dual Study Paths

Once generated, your lesson opens in the Lesson Overview screen, offering two paths to learn:

![Lesson Overview Screen](images/lesson_overview.png)

* **Review Now:** Launches active-recall flashcard study.
* **Concept Map:** Opens the interactive 2D relationship diagram.
* **Source Lines:** Every flashcard displays a **Source Lines** button. Tapping it shows the original note lines that justify the answer.

---

## 7. Active Recall with Flashcards & Leitner Spaced Repetition

Active recall is proven to strengthen long-term memory. NeuroScript AI organizes your cards using a 5-box Leitner scheduling system.

![Interactive Flashcard Review](images/flashcard_review.png)

* **Front of Card:** Displays the question or concept prompt.
* **Tap to Flip:** Tap the card to flip it and reveal the answer and source citation.
* **Rate Your Recall:**
  * **Incorrect / Hard (Red):** The card returns to **Box 1** to be reviewed again tomorrow.
  * **Correct / Mastered (Green):** The card moves up a box (Box 1 $\to$ 2 $\to$ 3 $\to$ 4 $\to$ 5). Higher boxes are reviewed less frequently (e.g., after 3, 7, 14, or 30 days).
* **ADHD Micro-Sessions:** If you are using the ADHD profile, your session automatically celebrates completion after 5 cards!

---

## 8. Visualizing with Concept Maps

If you are a visual learner, concept maps help you understand how different concepts connect.

![Interactive Concept Map](images/concept_map.png)

* **Interactive Canvas:** Use pinch-to-zoom and drag gestures to pan around the relationship diagram.
* **Connected Nodes:** Each bubble represents a key term, linked by labeled relationship arrows (e.g., *Chloroplast* $\to$ *houses* $\to$ *Thylakoids*).
* **Line Provenance:** Tapping a node highlights the original note line number where that idea was discussed.

---

## 9. Accessibility Settings & Customization

You can personalize your experience at any time by tapping **More options $\to$ Settings** from the Library.

![Settings and Fine-Tuning Screen](images/settings.png)

* **Switch Profile:** Switch between ADHD, Dyslexia, and Autism profiles with one tap.
* **Typography Scaling:** Adjust font size from 80% to 200% with immediate preview.
* **Reduced Motion:** Turn off bouncy animations and 3D card flips if you prefer static transitions.
* **High Contrast Outlines:** Adds bold borders around cards and buttons for higher visibility.
* **Security & Privacy:** View verified encryption status and clear local data whenever you choose.
